🔴

Critical Compliance Rules — Fertility / IVF Niche

  • — Never make absolute guarantees ("100% success", "guaranteed pregnancy") in any content or ad copy.
  • — Never use shame or urgency framing ("biological clock running out") — fertility audiences need hope, not panic or pressure.
  • — Never publish identifiable patient content without explicit written consent — documented every single time, no exceptions.
  • — Male infertility must be visible in content — 40%+ of infertility has a male factor. Do not produce female-only messaging.
  • — Audience is Marathi/Hindi bilingual — local platforms must include regional language content, not English only.
